How it works

No teacher, just the score counter

1P

Explore. 200 throwaway games generate 3,000 unique board positions — no expert player anywhere.

2P

Score. For every legal move the game engine itself prices the outcome: +10 a pellet, +200 a ghost, −500 getting caught, −1 for dawdling, and a nonsense move that isn't on the board earns the worst penalty of all.

3P

Reinforce (GRPO). On an A100, the model guesses 8 moves per board; moves that out-earn their siblings get reinforced. Same algorithm family DeepSeek used to train R1.

4P

Rematch. The trained model replays the identical 10 held-out mazes against the untouched base model.
